    Questions

    Short answers to what people ask most.

    Is StepUp free?

    Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, nothing to buy. Everything in the game is earned by walking.

    Which phones does it work on?

    Android 9 or newer with Health Connect (built into Android 14 and newer, a free app from Google Play on older phones). iPhone is not supported.

    Where do my steps come from?

    From Health Connect: your phone's own step counter, Google Fit, Samsung Health or a watch that syncs there. Steps typed in by hand don't count. The app has a step-by-step Google Fit guide.

    Why isn't it on Google Play?

    StepUp is in beta and shared straight from this page. Every file is signed with the StepUp key and its checksum is published here, so you can check it's genuine.

    Play Protect warns me. Is that safe?

    Google warns about apps it hasn't seen many times yet. Tap Scan app or Install without scanning. Only download StepUp from this page.

    How do I update?

    When a new version is out, the app tells you and its button brings you here. Install the new file over the old one; your progress is saved on your account.

    I got a new phone. What now?

    One account works on one phone. On the new phone sign in and tap Move my account: after 3 days it moves over, and your old phone can cancel it. You can move once every 30 days.

    What data does StepUp keep?

    Your email (for signing in, never shown), username, optional country, city and photo, and your daily step totals and game progress. Read the privacy page.

    How do I delete my account?

    In the app: Settings → Delete account. Your profile and progress are removed and the phone is freed.
