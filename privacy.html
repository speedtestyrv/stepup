Privacy

StepUp is a step game. This page says in plain words what the app keeps and why. Last updated November 2026.

What the app stores on its server

  • Your email and password, only to sign you in. Your email is never shown to other players.
  • Your profile: username, and if you add them, country, city and photo.
  • Steps and game progress: daily step totals, level, rank, streak, quests, chests, items, achievements and season results.
  • What you do with others: friends, chats with friends, duels, gifts, trades, clan membership and clan chat.
  • A phone code: a scrambled number made from your phone, so one account works on one phone. It can't be turned back into your phone's real IDs.
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes, the error, the app version, the phone model and Android version.
  • Reports you send about another player.

What other players see

Your username, photo, country and city, your steps and step history, level, rank, streak, achievements and clan. New accounts stay hidden from other players for a short time at first.

Steps and health data

StepUp reads only your step count from Health Connect. It never writes or changes health data and never reads anything else. Height and weight you enter for distance and calories stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

What StepUp doesn't do

  • No ads, no ad IDs, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No location: StepUp doesn't ask for GPS.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Where it's kept

On Google Firebase (sign-in and the Firestore database) in the European Union. Data travels encrypted.

Deleting your data

In the app open Settings → Delete account. Your profile and progress are removed and your phone is freed for a new account. You can also remove StepUp's step access at any time in Health Connect.

This website

This page sets no cookies, has no trackers and loads nothing from other sites. The download comes from GitHub, which hosts this page.
